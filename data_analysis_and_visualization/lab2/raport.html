Animated plots — World Bank population

Project spanning labs 1–3. Population data from the World Bank indicator SP.POP.TOTL (1960–2024) and land area AG.LND.TOTL.K2. Aggregate rows (World, OECD, income groups, …) are filtered out using the World Bank country metadata (real countries have a non-empty Region). All animations use matplotlib.animation; every script re-derives its own data.

Three country groups

  • (a) Five most populated in 2023: India, China, USA, Indonesia, Pakistan.
  • (b) Random group: a random country & year (seed-fixed) plus the four closest by population that year.
  • (c) Poland as centroid: Poland plus the four countries closest to it by population in 2023.

1. Bar plots — colour & black-and-white

Fixed population axis (bars grow, ranking is not rescaled), three-letter country codes tracking each bar, a large year counter and short 150M-style labels. The b&w versions use hatches instead of colour.

2. Line plots

Each series starts as a dot and grows into a line; the country code rides the leading end.

3. Bubble plots

x = year, y = population, bubble size = population density (people/km²), with a faded trajectory trail.

4. Other representation — ranking race

Horizontal bars re-sorted every year, so countries visibly overtake each other in rank (unlike the fixed vertical bars above).

5. Event highlight (lab 3, part III)

Rwanda and four neighbours. The animation freezes for ~4 seconds on 1994 with an explanatory label to emphasise the population collapse caused by the genocide (Rwanda fell from ~8.0M in 1993 to ~5.7M in 1995 before recovering).

6. Gantt chart (lab 3, part IV)

Organisation of the University of Warsaw 2026/2027 academic year for the Faculty of Mathematics, Computer Science and Mechanics, transcribed from the Rector's resolution M.2026.30.Post.1 (Appendix 1). Full-page A4 PDFs: colour / black-and-white.
